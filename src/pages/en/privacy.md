_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

This privacy notice applies to the Arqueozonas MX website, available at arqueozonas.mx (the "Site"), whose controller is Angel Marfil, domiciled in Cancún, Quintana Roo, Mexico.

## Personal data

The Site does not collect, store or process personal data from its users. It has no forms, user registration or any other means of requesting information, and it does not use cookies.

## Usage statistics

Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service that, according to the information published by its provider, uses no cookies or local storage, does not record IP addresses and does not track users across sites. Statistics are presented in aggregate form and cannot be used to identify users.

## Browser preferences

The color theme a user selects, if any, is saved in their browser's local storage. This value stays on their device, is not transmitted to the controller and can be deleted at any time from the browser settings.

## Third-party services and sites

The Site displays content provided by third-party services, such as hosting, maps and three-dimensional models, and includes links to external sites, such as virtual tours and official sources. Any data processing carried out by those third parties is governed by their own privacy notices, which users are encouraged to review.

## Changes to this privacy notice

Any amendment to this notice will be published on this page, together with the date of the last update.
