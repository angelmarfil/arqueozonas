## Description {#description}

The Yucatán Regional Museum of Anthropology, Palacio Cantón, preserves and promotes cultural heritage through exhibitions, research, conservation, and programs that foster dialogue, reflection, and community engagement.

## Historical overview {#history}

The Yucatán Regional Museum of Anthropology, Palacio Cantón, is housed in one of the most iconic buildings in the city of Mérida. Built in 1911, this historic building is part of Yucatán’s architectural heritage and has served the public since 1932.

The museum’s history at this location began in 1959, and it has since established itself as one of the most important cultural venues in southeastern Mexico and as a member of the Museum Network of the National Institute of Anthropology and History (INAH). Over the course of more than six decades, the museum has played a fundamental role in the research, conservation, restoration, and dissemination of the cultural heritage of Yucatán and Mexico. Its collection preserves artifacts of great value for understanding the cultural diversity and historical processes that have shaped the region. These collections have served as the basis for major national and international exhibitions and have contributed to the strengthening of various museums across the peninsula.

Today, the Palacio Cantón Museum is an organization in constant evolution. In addition to presenting temporary exhibitions and high-quality curatorial projects, it organizes academic and educational activities and engages with diverse audiences, promoting access to knowledge and the recognition of cultural heritage as a common good. The museum views heritage as a tool for fostering dialogue, reflection, and participation. To that end, it develops programs that strengthen relationships with communities, encourage the exchange of knowledge, and foster new perspectives on history, memory, identity, and cultural diversity.

Visiting Palacio Cantón means exploring a space where every exhibition, activity, and project seeks to spark curiosity, encourage critical thinking, and offer meaningful experiences for people of all ages.

The Yucatán Regional Museum of Anthropology is a gathering place for those who wish to learn about, understand, and appreciate our country’s cultural richness. Its doors remain open to welcome local, national, and international visitors, inviting them to discover the many stories that make up our heritage and to recognize that this legacy comes to life when it is shared, explored, preserved, and experienced collectively.
